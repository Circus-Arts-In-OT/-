About the Program

This website was created to highlight how circus arts can be used within occupational therapy to create engaging therapeutic experiences. Activities such as scarf juggling, balance games, creative movement, performance-based art, and group challenges can be adapted to meet the needs of children, older adults, and individuals with diverse abilities. This was an extension of a capstone project by Shayla Brammer, completing a capstone porject on circus arts and circus based activities in occupational therapy.

Areas Addressed

Motor Skills

Supports coordination, balance, reaching, hand-eye coordination, bilateral coordination, and endurance.

Cognition

Encourages attention, sequencing, problem-solving, memory, following directions, and motor planning.

Social Participation

Promotes peer interaction, turn-taking, teamwork, communication, and shared group experiences.

Confidence & Engagement

Builds self-expression, motivation, creativity, confidence, and willingness to participate in new activities.
