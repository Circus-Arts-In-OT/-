About the Program

This website was created to highlight how circus arts can be used within occupational therapy to create engaging therapeutic experiences. Activities such as scarf juggling, balance games, creative movement, performance-based art, and group challenges can be adapted to meet the needs of children, older adults, and individuals with diverse abilities. This was an extension of a capstone project by Shayla Brammer, completing a capstone porject on circus arts and circus based activities in occupational therapy.

Areas Addressed

Motor Skills

Circus arts activities can help support balance, coordination, bilateral coordination, crossing midline, posture, strength, and overall movement participation through fun and engaging activities.

Social Participation

Group-based circus activities encourage teamwork, communication, peer interaction, turn-taking, and participation within a supportive and inclusive environment.

Confidence & Self-Expression

Circus arts can promote creativity, self-expression, confidence, and willingness to try new activities while helping individuals experience success in meaningful ways.

Inclusive for All Abilities

Activities can be adapted and graded for children, older adults, wheelchair users, and individuals with diverse physical, cognitive, and sensory needs across the lifespan.

Engagement & Motivation

Circus arts provide a creative and motivating approach that may increase participation, attention, enjoyment, and overall engagement during therapeutic activities.

Creative Occupational Therapy

This program highlights how occupational therapists can explore innovative and community-based interventions to support meaningful participation, wellness, and quality of life.
